A voice assistant that runs in your browser. Say “Hey Mo” and then ask.

Press Start, allow the microphone, then say “Hey Mo”.

    Voice

    Dictation

    Book reader

    No book loaded. Choose a PDF or text file you have the right to use (on a phone, pick it from Files, Downloads or Drive). It stays in your browser and is not uploaded.

    Try saying

    • Hey Mo, what is adenocarcinoma
    • Hey Mo, set a timer for 5 minutes
    • Hey Mo, what time is it
    • Hey Mo, search for weather in Ipoh
    • Hey Mo, open YouTube
    • Hey Mo, tell me a joke
    • Hey Mo, start dictation
    • Hey Mo, what does the book say about …
    • Hey Mo, read the book / read page 3

    Works in Chrome and Edge. Your browser’s speech service turns your voice into text (Chrome sends the audio to Google), so don’t dictate patient names or other identifying details. Definitions are brief general information, not clinical advice.
